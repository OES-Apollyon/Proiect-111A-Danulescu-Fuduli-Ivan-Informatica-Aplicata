Our Products

EUV lithography systems

Our extreme ultraviolet (EUV) lithography systems provide the highest resolution in high-volume manufacturing. By enabling chipmakers to squeeze even more transistors onto a single chip, the state-of-the-art technology is pushing Moore's Law forward.

DUV lithography systems

Deep ultraviolet (DUV) lithography systems are the cornerstone of the semiconductor industry. The dry and immersion DUV systems in our portfolio are used for high-volume manufacturing of the most advanced Logic and Memory chips, novel chip innovation and everything in between.

Metrology and inspection systems

Delivering speed and accuracy, our metrology and inspection portfolio covers every step in the chip manufacturing process, from RnD to mass production.

Computational lithography

Starlight Electronics is developing upon this industry-leading technique, which minimizes the physical and chemical effects that interfere with the quality of a chip.
