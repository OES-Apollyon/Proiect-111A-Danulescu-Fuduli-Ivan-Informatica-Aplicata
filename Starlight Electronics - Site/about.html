Our Story

Starlight Electronics was founded on a simple but ambitious principle: to engineer the tools that power the future. From our humble beginnings in an old factory in Bucharest to becoming a global leader in semiconductor technology, our journey has always been driven not only by precision and innovation but also a wish to offer the people of the world access to high quality electronics at very competitive prices.

Over the past decade, we have dedicated ourselves to pushing the boundaries of what is physically possible. By developing advanced lithography systems and in-house microchips, we enable our partners to create smaller, faster, and more efficient devices that improve everyday life.

Our team of world-class engineers, researchers, and visionaries work tirelessly to solve complex challenges. We believe that the most significant technological leaps are born from meticulous research and a relentless pursuit of perfection. Every microscopic detail matters.

As we look to the future, Starlight Electronics remains committed to sustainable growth and groundbreaking innovation. We are not just manufacturing components; we are building the foundation of tomorrow's electronic world.
